◉ LIVE EARTH — FIELD MANUAL

A real-time 3D command center: world news, earthquakes, satellites, air traffic and live TV on a photoreal globe with a true day/night terminator.

HOTKEYS

  • N news events   Q earthquakes   S satellites   T TV pins   F air traffic
  • U cities   A aurora oval   W rain radar (2D)   C clouds   L night lights
  • B borders   R auto-rotate   I click intel   M 2D map   ? manual   ESC close

MOUSE

  • Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · click a marker for details
  • Click a city dot or label for a full live dossier: local time, weather, air quality, Wikipedia brief
  • Click open ocean/land for surface weather intel (toggleable)
  • Click a satellite dot to select it and see its orbit trail

DATA SOURCES (all free, no keys)

  • News: GDELT Project GEO 2.0 (updated ~15 min)
  • Earthquakes: USGS 24h feed · Satellites: CelesTrak TLE + SGP4
  • Air traffic: OpenSky Network (anonymous, rate-limited)
  • Aurora + Kp: NOAA SWPC OVATION · Rain radar: RainViewer
  • Weather/air quality: Open-Meteo · City briefs: Wikipedia · Markets: CoinGecko

If a source is unreachable you'll see amber DEMO indicators — the app never breaks, it degrades to demo data.
